A Message from Chloe Basiima

“The CBC Life Conference isn't just an event; it's a movement. It's a gathering of minds ready to challenge the status quo, share authentic stories, and build a foundation for the future.”

We are bringing together thought leaders, creatives, and industry experts to dive deep into the intersections of Career, Business, and Faith. In a world full of noise, we are carving out a space for truth, vulnerability, and real transformation.

Whether you are an aspiring entrepreneur, a seasoned professional, or simply looking for your tribe, this conference is designed to equip you with the tools and authentic connections you need to thrive. Let's shape the future of Africa, together.
