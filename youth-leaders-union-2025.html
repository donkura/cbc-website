Youth Leaders Union 2025

Organised by EduTourism for Unity

At CBC, our mission goes beyond the screen. We believe in the power of real conversations to drive real change. Our involvement in international speaking opportunities, such as the Youth Leaders Union 2025 organized by EduTourism for Unity, reflects our commitment to bringing African voices to global stages.

Through these engagements, we bridge communities, spark honest dialogue, and empower the next generation of leaders to share their truth and transform society.
