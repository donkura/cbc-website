“Every voice matters. When we create safe spaces for honest dialogue, real healing and community transformation begin.”

Chloe Basiima is a storyteller, community builder, and the visionary behind Africa's most anticipated conversation platform. Born from a deep belief that every voice matters, she launched CBC to break the silence that holds communities back.

With roots in Uganda and a vision for the continent, Chloe blends emotional intelligence with strategic thinking — creating spaces where truth is welcomed, stories heal, and communities transform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About CBC

Find answers to common questions about our platform, episodes, founder Chloe Basiima, and partnership opportunities.

What is CBC (The Chloe Basiima Covert)? +

CBC is Africa's premier conversation platform created to foster open, safe, and dynamic spaces for honest dialogue. Through intimate studio recordings, community healing corners, and youth mentorship forums, CBC empowers individuals to turn real-life pain into purpose and transformative social change.

Who is the founder and host of CBC? +

CBC was founded and is hosted by Chloe Basiima — a visionary Ugandan storyteller, keynote speaker, and youth advocate committed to breaking societal silences across Africa and equipping emerging leaders.

Where can I stream full CBC episodes? +

You can stream full video episodes directly on our Episodes Page, subscribe to our official YouTube channel, and follow short video reels on Instagram and TikTok.

How can brands, NGOs, or individuals partner with CBC? +

We welcome collaborations with purpose-aligned organizations, corporate brands, and creative partners. Visit our Partner Page or send inquiries through our Contact Page.

What is the CBC Life Conference 2026? +

The CBC Life Conference is our signature annual gathering in Kampala, Uganda bringing together changemakers, youth, and leaders to explore key pillars in Career, Business, and Faith. Learn more on our Conference Details Page.
